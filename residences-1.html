Inside the homes

The building sits near the interstate and the rail line, so it was built past standard: double walls between homes and extra insulation between floors. Inside, it’s calm.

  • KitchensQuartz countertops, tile backsplash, 42-inch slow-close cabinets and a full appliance package.
  • BathsTiled showers with glass doors. Homes with a second full bath also have a tub.
  • LaundryA washer and dryer in every home.
  • RoomsNine-foot ceilings, ceiling fans, walk-in closets and a window in every bedroom.
  • FinishesThree finish schemes to choose from across the building.
  • BuildingTwo elevators, on-site parking, and more parking for lease across Cafego Place.
  • ViewsThe Sunsphere, Sharp’s Ridge, House Mountain and the downtown skyline.
