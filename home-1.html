Questions buyers ask

Read all the answers
Are condos available now?

Yes. Studio, one-bedroom and two-bedroom homes are available to buy today. Availability changes as homes sell, so contact us for current inventory and pricing.

What are the HOA fees, and what do they cover?

Dues range from $132 to $370 per month in the first year, depending on the size of the home. They cover water, trash, inspections, preventative maintenance, annual testing, repairs and day-to-day management of the building.

What parking is available?

An on-site lot is managed by the HOA, which will decide whether spaces are first-come, first-served or leased to owners. A second lot across Cafego Place offers monthly parking through ParkTenn. Together they provide nearly one space per home. Guests can use nearby street parking, garages and public lots.

Is financing available?

City Summit doesn’t offer in-house financing, but we can recommend trusted local lenders who finance downtown Knoxville condos.
