About City Summit

89 condos at one of the highest points in downtown Knoxville, built for people who want to own a home in the center of the city.

A downtown landmark

City Summit sits at one of the highest points in downtown Knoxville. From the balconies and living rooms you can see the Sunsphere, Sharp’s Ridge and House Mountain, with the skyline in between.

It’s part of one of the fastest-growing pockets of downtown, alongside Summit Towers, Ryan’s Row and City House, and a short walk from Market Square and the Old City.

Priced for Knoxville

Our goal was homes near the median price for greater Knoxville. Downtown, that means a smaller footprint, and a real choice: a larger home outside the center, or a smaller one in the heart of it, at a comparable price.

Who built it

City Summit was developed by LawlerWood, built by Merit Construction and designed by Design Innovation Architects. Where we could, we sourced locally; the windows and door units come from Dealer’s Warehouse here in Knoxville.

LawlerWood is a Knoxville real estate development and property management firm founded by Rodney Lawler and Pat Wood, with nearly 50 years of experience and two billion dollars in completed development and management projects.

Questions buyers ask

Are condos available now?

Yes. Studio, one-bedroom and two-bedroom homes are available to buy today. Availability changes as homes sell, so contact us for current inventory and pricing.

What are the HOA fees, and what do they cover?

Dues range from $132 to $370 per month in the first year, depending on the size of the home. They cover water, trash, inspections, preventative maintenance, annual testing, repairs and day-to-day management of the building.

Which utilities do I set up myself?

Electric and internet. Electric service is through KUB, and every home is pre-wired for both KUB Fiber and Comcast.

What parking is available?

An on-site lot is managed by the HOA, which will decide whether spaces are first-come, first-served or leased to owners. A second lot across Cafego Place offers monthly parking through ParkTenn. Together they provide nearly one space per home. Guests can use nearby street parking, garages and public lots.

Can I rent out my condo?

Yes, under the HOA guidelines. Homes on every floor may be leased for 90 days or longer. First-floor homes are also eligible for short-term rentals of less than 90 days, subject to the HOA governing documents and City of Knoxville regulations.

Are pets allowed?

Yes. Breed, weight and other pet requirements are set out in the HOA governing documents, which every owner receives before closing.

Does every home include a washer and dryer?

Yes, every home comes with both.

Is financing available?

City Summit doesn’t offer in-house financing, but we can recommend trusted local lenders who finance downtown Knoxville condos.

Is City Summit a good fit for a UT student?

It can be. It isn’t student housing, but it’s minutes from the University of Tennessee campus, Cumberland Avenue and Neyland Stadium, and many families want something more than a typical student apartment.
